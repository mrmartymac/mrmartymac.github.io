Import settings for Blox to Scoop import.

1. Identify the letter codes for Product Names in Blox.
2. Assign the three letter codes to Products.
3. Identify the Templates used.
4. Create matching Templates in Scoop.
5. Assign the templates to Products.
6. Add entry to the Template Map in Product setup to define the basket the template should go to. The Section Name in this case it not important.
